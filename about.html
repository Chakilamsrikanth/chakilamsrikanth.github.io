Little about me

I’m a Software Engineer who enjoys turning complex problems into reliable, easy-to-use applications. I work across frontend interfaces, backend services, databases, and cloud infrastructure, with Java, Go, Python, React, and Spring Boot as core tools. I care about writing maintainable code, designing secure APIs, and understanding how each part of a system affects the people using it. My work also includes integrating AI into applications, including LLM powered search and information lookup. I’m interested in making these features useful in practice, with attention to security, performance, and how they fit into existing workflows. With a master’s degree in Applied Computer Science and an interest in visual design, I bring both a technical foundation and an eye for usability to what I build.
